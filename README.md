# SQL Data Analytics Projects

This repository contains two SQL course-end projects focused on analyzing operational and organizational data to support practical business decisions.

## 1. Air Cargo Analysis

### Scope

Analyzes airline customer, passenger travel, ticket sales, and route data using the AirCargo database. The project works with customer, passengers_on_flights, ticket_details, and routes data to examine travel activity, ticket revenue, airline brands, and route performance.

### What It Is Used For

- Identify regular customers for targeted offers and customer retention.
- Examine passenger traffic across routes and identify busy routes.
- Support decisions about aircraft allocation using route and passenger activity.
- Analyze passenger counts and ticket revenue by travel class.
- Review ticket prices, airline brands, and customer booking patterns.
- Prepare ticket sales and route-level summaries for operational analysis.

[Open Air Cargo Analysis](Project-1-Air-Cargo-Analysis/)

## 2. Employee Performance Mapping

### Scope

Analyzes employee, department, performance, salary, experience, and project-assignment data using SQL. The project examines employee records and job profiles to understand workforce distribution and compare employee attributes against organizational requirements.

### What It Is Used For

- Retrieve and organize employee information by department.
- Evaluate employee performance ratings and experience.
- Compare salary levels across roles, countries, and continents.
- Validate employee job profiles against experience requirements.
- Calculate employee bonuses using salary and performance ratings.
- Support HR reporting and workforce analysis.

[Open Employee Performance Mapping](Project-2-Employee-Performance-Mapping/)
